# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) —
the canonical text, with examples of acceptable and unacceptable behavior, lives at
that link. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.

## Contact

Concerns or reports go through [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/rafachavantes/jax-os/security/advisories/new), GitHub's private reporting channel for this repository. If you publish your own copy, point this link at your own repository.
